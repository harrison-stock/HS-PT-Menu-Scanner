We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Emails

We only send marketing emails if you ticked the box at sign-up or turned emails on in Settings. They're occasional training and nutrition tips, including offers on Harrison's coaching. Every email has a way to unsubscribe, and you can switch them off in Settings at any time.

Who else handles it

These companies process information for us, under contracts that only let them use it to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in. Holds your account, profile, scan counts and email preference, and keeps security logs of sign-ins, including IP addresses.
  • Vercel: hosts the app and runs the part that talks to the AI. Its request logs include IP addresses.
  • Anthropic: the AI (Claude) that reads menus. It receives the menu and your goal, calorie target and restrictions, not your name or email. Under its commercial terms it doesn't train its models on this and keeps it only for a short period. Anthropic is in the United States, and transfers there are covered by the safeguards in its data processing terms.
  • Cloudflare: the security check on sign-in and sign-up, which looks at your browser to tell people from bots.
  • Google Fonts: serves the app's fonts, so your browser's address is seen by Google when it loads them.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and profile: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Scan counts: about two days.
  • Menu photos and text: not kept by us at all.
  • Consent records: as long as your account exists, so we can show what you agreed to. They go when your account does.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent at any time. Most of it you can change yourself in Settings. For anything else, including deleting your account, email harrison@harrisonstock.co.uk and we'll reply within a month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We'd appreciate the chance to sort it out first.
